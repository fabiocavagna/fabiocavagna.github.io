## Objective
The project presents the conceptual design and performance estimation of JUPITER V, a two-stage solid-propellant suborbital sounding rocket carrying a 50 kg capsule for microgravity experiments to at least 200 km altitude. The design prioritizes consolidated (high-TRL) technologies with only minor adaptations.
