**Objective:** Study of the structural compliance of an ideal axial-symmetric spacecraft with the Vega-C launcher
**Tools:** MSC Nastran, Femap, MATLAB
**Approach:** The following analyses were carried out: 
◇ Fairing usable volume limitations
◇ Mass/CoG limitations
◇ Modal analysis
◇ Static analysis and overfluxes
◇ Launcher/Spacecraft dynamic coupling
◇ Thermoelastic analysis
◇ Sine test prediction (with primary and secondary nothing)
◇ Coupled Load Analysis (Shock Response Spectrum and Equivalent Sine Input)
◇ Acoustic test prediction
◇ Severity comparison (sine vs acoustic vs shock)
◇ Buckling analysis
